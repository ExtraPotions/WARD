# <img src="assets/ward-launcher.svg" width="48" height="48" alt=""> WARD

Hide or dim sponsored placements, upsells and urgency messages on supported stores.

[Install WARD](https://github.com/ExtraPotions/WARD/releases/latest/download/ward.user.js) · Requires [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/get-it/) or [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).

After installing, refresh a supported store and open the WARD gem. [Setup and controls →](docs/usage.md)

## Features

- Choose protection levels and controls for each store.
- Reveal hidden content, allow a pattern or pause WARD.
- Recolor store pages while preserving product photos and purchase information.
- Review Amazon seller details and optionally clip eligible coupons.

Supports Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, Target, Best Buy and Shopify stores. Essential purchasing information stays visible; seller hints do not judge quality or authenticity.
