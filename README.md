# WARD

**Less shopping pressure, more room to decide.**

## Get started

1. Install a userscript manager such as [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/get-it/) or [Tampermonkey](https://www.tampermonkey.net/).
2. [Install WARD](https://github.com/ExtraPotions/WARD/releases/latest/download/ward.user.js) and confirm in your userscript manager.
3. Refresh a supported store and open the WARD launcher.

## What you can do

- **Calm the page:** hide or dim sponsored placements, upsells, urgency messages, and pushy recommendations at the level you choose. Essential purchasing information stays visible.
- **Shop six stores:** Amazon, eBay, Etsy, Walmart, Target, and Best Buy, each with its own controls.
- **Undo changes:** reveal hidden content, allow a pattern on a page, or pause WARD entirely.
- **See who you're buying from:** on Amazon, view the brand, seller, and fulfillment, with optional hints about unusual sellers or ratings. These hints stay on your device and don't judge quality or authenticity.
- **Clip coupons:** optionally clip eligible coupons automatically.
